🎓 Quantitative Math Concepts

1. Moving Average (MA)

Smoothes out daily noise to reveal trend direction. A 20-MA crossing above a 50-MA is called a Golden Cross signal.

2. Sharpe Ratio Formula

\( Sharpe = \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p} \)

Higher Sharpe ratio = better return for every dollar of risk taken!

3. Quantitative NLP Sentiment

\( S = \frac{\sum w_{\text{pos}} - \sum w_{\text{neg}}}{\sqrt{(\sum w)^2 + 15}} \)
