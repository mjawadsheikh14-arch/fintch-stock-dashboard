🎓 Quantitative Math Concepts

1. Moving Average (MA)

Smoothes out daily noise to reveal trend direction. A 20-MA crossing above a 50-MA is called a Golden Cross signal.

2. Sharpe Ratio Formula

\( Sharpe = \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p} \)

Higher Sharpe ratio = better return for every dollar of risk taken!

3. Quantitative NLP Sentiment

\( S = \frac{\sum w_{\text{pos}} - \sum w_{\text{neg}}}{\sqrt{(\sum w)^2 + 15}} \)

Financial dictionary scoring news headlines in [-1.0, +1.0] with negation detection and intensifiers.

4. Geometric Brownian Motion (Monte Carlo)

\( S_{t+\Delta t} = S_t \exp\left(\left(\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)\Delta t + \sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} Z\right) \)

Simulating stochastic price paths where \(Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\) models daily market shocks. Computes 95% Value at Risk (VaR) and distribution cones.

5. Markowitz Modern Portfolio Theory

\( \sigma_p = \sqrt{\mathbf{w}^T \boldsymbol{\Sigma} \mathbf{w}}, \quad \mathbb{E}[R_p] = \mathbf{w}^T \boldsymbol{\mu} \)

Nobel Prize model proving that asset covariance \(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\) cancels individual volatility, revealing the optimal Efficient Frontier.
